# Active Bayesian local-line target comparison

Only `b_bayesian_local_line_mV_dec` is active. The KDE uses all 97 target
values. The literature comparison uses the 68 records with an article-reported
Tafel slope; the 29 records without one are excluded rather than replaced by a
reconstruction. Records 42 and 43 share the legacy Record 42 reported
comparator of 95 mV dec-1.

The scatter uses a perfect-agreement line with nested +/-10% and +/-20%
relative-error bands and retains the full observed range. Records 23 and 74
use the registered quantization-aware PAVA fallback after the strict
progression selector found no supported interval.

PNG at 300 dpi is the only active figure format. The former PDF copies are
recoverable under
`archive/project_cleanup_pre_presto_policy_v2_complete_20260730/`.
