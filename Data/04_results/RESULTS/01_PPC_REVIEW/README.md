2026-07-23 and approved all 97/97 PPCs. Records 23 and 74 use the registered
quantization-aware PAVA fallback after the strict observed-progression

local-line estimand; it does not substitute a selected-model or Equation-2
value. The current decisions were entered for this evidence package and were
